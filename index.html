Mission Briefing

Beginner's blueprint

01 · The 12 notes

A A# B C C# D D# E F F# G G# then repeat. Each fret is one step up. E–F and B–C have no sharp between them.

02 · Learning sequence

  • Open chords: C A G E D + basic strumming
  • Smooth chord changes to the metronome
  • Minor pentatonic, Box 1: your first solo tool
  • Barre chords (E and A shapes) unlock the neck
  • Major scale and triads

03 · Why scales

They show which notes belong together, build dexterity and give you material to improvise. Pentatonics are 5-note scales that rarely sound wrong, which is why rock, blues and pop lean on them.

04 · Study the masters

  • Hendrix: minor pentatonic plus thumb-over chords
  • B.B. King: the blues box, vibrato and bends
  • Frusciante: triads and CAGED
  • Joe Pass: chord-melody

05 · Practice protocol

  • 15 minutes daily beats 3 hours weekly
  • Always use the metronome
  • Squeeze a barre, release tension, squeeze again

Daily 15: 5m chords · 5m scales · 5m songs.
